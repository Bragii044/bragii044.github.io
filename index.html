🖥️ Micro-Multi - Your Local AI Team, Simplified

Micro-Multi is a desktop workspace where several AI helpers work side by side on your own computer. No cloud account, no confusing setup. This page walks you through downloading it on Windows in plain, everyday language.

⬇️ Download Micro-Multi for Windows

Official download page · Free to use

✨ What Is Micro-Multi?

Most AI tools give you one assistant in a browser tab. Micro-Multi gives you a small team of them, sitting right inside a desktop window on your machine.

Think of an "agent" as a helper with a specific job. One agent might research a topic, another might organize what it found, and a third might write the final draft. Micro-Multi lets those helpers share a workspace so you can watch the work happen and step in whenever you want.

The application is local-first. That means your files, notes, and project folders stay on your computer rather than being shipped off to someone else's server. It is also compatible with deepseek-harness (dsh) plugins, which is a common way for this kind of software to gain extra abilities.

In short: you get a friendly desktop home for AI helpers that cooperate instead of competing.

🧩 What Makes It Different

  • A team, not a single chat. Several AI agents can work in the same workspace, each handling its own part of a task while you keep an eye on the whole picture.
  • Your computer is the home base. Micro-Multi is built to run as a desktop application, so it feels like a normal program rather than a website you have to keep logging into.
  • Plugin friendly. The workspace is compatible with the deepseek-harness (dsh) plugin system, so the abilities you already know from that world can carry over.
  • Skills that add up. Small, reusable skills let you teach the workspace a repeatable routine once and use it again and again.
  • Built for connecting things. Support for MCP (a common connector standard) means the workspace can reach out to the tools you already rely on.
  • Local-first by design. Your work lives on your machine, which keeps things private and keeps you in control.

💡 Everyday Ways to Use It

You do not need to be a programmer to get value out of a multi-agent workspace. Here are ordinary things people use it for:

  • Turning a messy pile of research notes into a tidy summary.
  • Drafting an email, a report, or a plan while one agent checks facts and another cleans up the wording.
  • Sorting and renaming a folder of files that has gotten out of hand.
  • Brainstorming options for a project and having several helpers argue the pros and cons.
  • Breaking a big task into smaller steps so nothing gets forgotten.
  • Keeping a personal knowledge folder that answers questions in your own words.

🔌 Works With dsh Plugins

If you have ever added an extension to a browser, you already understand plugins. They are small add-ons that teach a program new tricks.

Micro-Multi is compatible with the deepseek-harness (dsh) plugin system. That means plugins built for that ecosystem can be used inside the workspace, so you are not starting from zero. If a plugin already does the thing you need, you can bring it along.

For anyone who prefers keeping their setup consistent, this compatibility is the quiet superpower of the app: the same habits, the same add-ons, but inside a friendlier desktop workspace.

🛡️ Your Files Stay Yours

"Local-first" is one of those phrases that sounds technical but means something very simple: the program runs on your computer, and your documents stay there too.

You decide which folders the workspace can see. You decide what gets shared inside a task. Nothing disappears into a mystery account you cannot inspect, and you can always open your own files with the programs you already use.

For anyone handling personal notes, client documents, or work that should not wander, that peace of mind is worth more than a long feature list.
