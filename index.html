Kain and the Book of Keepings

Cover art by Colin Laurel

Sylva trusts the old stories as little as she trusts Lethescrest’s crooked high priest. But when her beloved guardian falls dangerously ill, she does the unthinkable and invokes a forgotten prayer.

The Wanderer has no memory when he’s escorted into Lethescrest by Dag—a bull-man who doesn’t know he ushers an ancient curse into the village. The village goddess manifests at The Wanderer’s presence, and what Sylva feared becomes known to all: Death, the mountain god, has answered her prayer.

Read more

Exiled and unlikely allies, the three are thrust into the underbelly of a corrupt pantheon on the brink of civil war. To survive, they must each reckon with what haunts them: the power Death is offering Sylva, the primordial force laying claim to Dag’s bloodline, and the sins of mankind’s first murderer catching up at last.

Because if they can’t break the cycle of judgment, history won’t just repeat itself.

Death will finish what he started.

Kain and the Book of Keepings reimagines Genesis and Revelation as bookends to the same story—where gods, mankind, and myth were born, and will ultimately conclude. And just possibly… begin again.

About Tyler

  • Home The West Coast

Tyler Wickstrom writes at the intersection of religious systems, mythology, and epic adventure. A former campus staff minister turned sustainability engineer turned creator, they believe that if we become the stories we tell ourselves, we craft not only our marvels, but our monsters too.

They enjoy hosting a local writing community and exploring the West Coast they call home.
